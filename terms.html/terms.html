1. Appointments

Appointments are subject to availability and are confirmed after the booking process has been completed.

Customers are encouraged to arrive approximately 5 to 10 minutes before their scheduled appointment.

3. Late Arrivals

Arriving late may reduce the amount of time available for the selected service. Significant delays may require the appointment to be rescheduled.

4. Cancellations

Customers are encouraged to cancel or reschedule appointments as early as possible.

5. Services

Service descriptions and prices displayed on the website are subject to change. Customers will be informed of significant changes before services are provided.

6. Payments

Payment is due after the service unless another payment arrangement has been agreed upon.

7. Customer Information

Information submitted through the booking form should be accurate and up to date so that we can communicate appointment information.

8. Calendar Events

Calendar files and calendar links are provided as a convenience. Customers remain responsible for checking that the appointment has been successfully added to their preferred calendar.

We aim to keep website information accurate and current. Service availability, prices, opening hours and other information may change.
